Why your choices matter

Car → metro, bus or carpool
Fewer vehicles means less exhaust and road dust. Traffic is a major local source of fine particles (PM2.5) in cities, so each swapped trip helps your own street air.
Not burning waste or leaves
Open burning releases dense smoke at ground level where families breathe it. Skipping a single burn removes a source right next to your home.
Induction or LPG instead of wood or coal
Solid-fuel cooking is one of the largest sources of indoor PM2.5. Cleaner fuel lowers exposure for the person cooking and everyone indoors.
Ventilating at the right time
Air is usually worst in early morning and late evening in winter. Airing rooms at midday dilutes indoor pollutants without letting in the peak.
Well-fitted N95/FFP2 mask
Cloth and surgical masks barely stop fine particles. A snug N95 filters most of them, but only if sealed against the face.
Sharing what you learn
Smog is a shared problem. Neighbours who skip burning, use cleaner fuel and avoid idling add up to cleaner air for the whole lane.
